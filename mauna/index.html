Privacy policy

Questions

Anything else? Write to support@buddhabytes.app.

Will I lose access to my apps?

No. Every app is still one quick search away. Mauna only adds a gentle pause before the ones you mark as noisy.

Will I miss important notifications?

Calls, alarms, one-time codes and the people you name always come through straight away.

Is Mauna for iPhone?

Mauna is an Android launcher. iPhone doesn't allow replacing the home screen.
