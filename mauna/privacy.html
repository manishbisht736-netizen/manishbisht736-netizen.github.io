Mauna Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026 · App: Mauna (Android home-screen launcher) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Mauna is a calm home screen for Android. Everything Mauna knows about you stays on your phone. Mauna has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else. The Android app does not even request internet access.

What Mauna stores on your phone

  • Your app list: the names and icons of apps installed on your phone that can be opened from a home screen, so Mauna can show and open them. Mauna asks Android only for launchable apps (it does not use the "all apps" permission).
  • Your settings: your chosen theme, language, Essential Seven apps, which apps you marked as noisy, hidden or renamed apps, your daily intention, your calm goal, digest times and the names you added to "People who always come through".
  • Your calm counts: per day, how many apps you opened from Mauna, how many were noisy, how often the Breath Gate appeared and how often you chose "Not now", seeds earned, Vine rounds played, and your weekly calm rating.
  • Senior Mode (optional): family contact names, phone numbers and photos you add, your SOS number and an optional settings PIN.

Notification access (optional, for Calm Digest)

If you turn on Calm Digest and allow notification access, Android lets Mauna see notifications as they arrive. Mauna only acts on notifications from the apps you chose to hold. For those, it saves the app name, the notification title and text and the time on your phone, removes the notification from your notification shade, and shows it again at your digest times (three times a day by default). Calls, alarms, navigation, reminders, ongoing notifications, one-time codes and messages whose title includes a name you added always come through normally. Held notifications are deleted after 7 days or when you mark them as seen. Mauna never sends notification content anywhere and never uses it for advertising. You can turn off notification access at any time in Android Settings.

Usage access (optional, for Sunday Reflection)

If you allow usage access, Mauna reads, on your phone, how many times your phone was unlocked and which apps you used most this week, to show estimated pickups and screen time in your weekly Reflection. Mauna shows this information and does not keep it or send it anywhere.

Other permissions

  • Home app: you choose Mauna as your home screen in Android. You can switch back at any time in Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app.
  • Notifications: used to tell you when your digest is ready and when a session you chose ("open for 10 minutes") is over.
  • Start at boot: used only to re-schedule your digest times after the phone restarts.
  • Phone: the keypad, family tiles and SOS open your phone app with the number filled in. Mauna cannot place calls itself.
  • Photos: only if you add a family photo in Senior Mode. The photo is resized and kept on your phone.

Mauna does not use Accessibility services.

Sharing and selling

We do not collect your data, so we cannot share or sell it. There are no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or crash reporting in Mauna.

Your choices and deleting your data

In Mauna → Settings → Privacy you can export your Mauna data as a file or erase all of it. Uninstalling Mauna also deletes everything it stored. Because there is no account, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

Mauna is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Wellbeing information

Mauna is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Study results mentioned in the app are summarised with their limits and may not apply to everyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. If a future version of Mauna ever needs to send data off your phone, we will explain it here and in the app before it happens.
