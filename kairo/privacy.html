Kairo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · App: Kairo (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Kairo is a day planner and focus timer. It has no ads and no analytics. By default everything you put into Kairo is stored only on your phone. If you choose to sign in for team sync, only the tasks and lists you share with other people are stored on our server so those people can see them (details below). We never sell your data.

Information stored on your phone

Kairo saves the following only in the app's private storage on your device: tasks, projects, notes, dates and estimates; focus sessions and the thoughts you park during them; daily check-ins (energy level, an optional mood word and your "win for today"); shutdown and weekly-review answers; program reflections; and your settings (theme, day hours, reminder times, comfort modes). We cannot see or access any of it.

Sharing tasks with other people

Without signing in: when you assign a task to someone, Kairo prepares an email, chat message or calendar file on your phone and hands it to the app you choose (for example your email app or WhatsApp). We do not receive it.

With team sync (optional sign-in with your email): we store your email address, the name and colour you chose, and only the tasks and lists you share: a task you assign to someone, a task assigned to you, or any task in a list that has members. For those we store the task details (title, notes, dates, times, status, hurdle, next step, steps, who it is assigned to and by whom, and its change history), comments on them, and the email addresses of list members. Tasks you don't share never leave your phone. We use this only to show shared tasks to the people involved and to email them about assignments, status changes, comments and list invitations. Emails are sent through our email provider Resend; sign-in codes are sent through Supabase with Resend. Data is stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India, and encrypted in transit.

People you share with can see the shared tasks, the comments, and your name and email. You can delete your account at any time in Me → Account & sharing → Delete my Kairo account; this removes your shared tasks, lists, comments and profile from our server. Email support@buddhabytes.app for help.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): the daily reminders you choose (morning plan, shutdown, one nudge, weekly review), task reminders you set, the end of a focus timer, and alerts when someone assigns you a task. Reminders are scheduled on the phone.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback when you finish a task or a timer. You can turn it off in Settings.

Kairo does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

Sharing and export

If you tap Share or Export, Kairo opens Android's share sheet with a text summary or a JSON file of your data. Where it goes next is your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes everything from the phone immediately, and uninstalling does the same. If you used team sync, also use Me → Account & sharing → Delete my Kairo account (or email us) to remove what is stored on our server.

Children

Kairo is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Health information

Kairo is a productivity tool, not a medical device. Focus, energy and break suggestions are general patterns, not medical advice, and Kairo does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

Changes

If we add features that send more data (for example calendar sync, backup or AI suggestions), we will update this policy and the Play Store Data safety section before that version is released, and those features will be optional.
