Kairo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 (replaces the version of 28 September 2026) · App: Kairo: To Do List & Planner (Android, iPhone and web at buddhabytes.app/kairo/app) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Kairo is a to-do list, planner and focus timer. It has no ads and no analytics, and we never sell your data. You sign in with your email so your tasks stay in sync between your phone and your laptop and so you can share tasks with other people. Your tasks are stored on our server for that purpose only.

What we store on our server

  • Account: your email address, the name and colour you choose, and an optional profile photo.
  • Your tasks and lists: titles, notes, dates, times, deadlines, priority, status, steps, hurdles, next steps, who a task is assigned to and by whom, and its change history; your lists and their members' email addresses; comments; and photos you add to comments.

We use this only to run Kairo: to show your tasks on your devices, to show shared tasks to the people you share them with, and to email people about assignments, status changes, comments and list invitations. Data is stored with Supabase (Mumbai, India) and encrypted in transit. Sign-in codes and notification emails are sent through our email provider Resend. Profile and comment photos are stored as small WebP images; anyone with a photo's link can open it, so don't add photos you want kept private.

What stays on your device

Focus sessions and the thoughts you park during them, daily check-ins (energy level and "win for today"), shutdown and weekly-review answers, program reflections and your settings (theme, day hours, reminder times, comfort modes) stay in the app's private storage on your device.

Who can see your tasks

Only you, unless you share: people you assign a task to, and members of a list or team you add, can see those tasks, their comments and photos, and your name, email and profile photo. Tasks you don't share are visible only to you.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you tap "Google Calendar" on a task, Kairo opens Google Calendar with that task's title, time and notes filled in so you can save it. Kairo does not read your calendar and does not receive anything back from Google.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): task reminders, alerts at a task's time and on deadline day, the daily reminders you choose, the end of a focus timer, and alerts when someone assigns you a task. Reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Photos (optional): only when you choose a photo for your profile or a comment.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Kairo does not use your location, contacts, microphone or health data.

Sharing and export

Share and Export open your device's share sheet with a text summary or a JSON file of your data; where it goes next is your choice.

Deleting your data

Me → Account & sharing → Delete my Kairo account removes your account, tasks, lists, comments and profile from our server; see buddhabytes.app/kairo/delete-data.html. Settings → Your data → Delete all data clears the device. Email support@buddhabytes.app for help.

Children

Kairo is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Health information

Kairo is a productivity tool, not a medical device. Focus, energy and break suggestions are general patterns, not medical advice, and Kairo does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

Changes

If we change what Kairo sends, we update this policy and the store privacy answers before that version is released.
