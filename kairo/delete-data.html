Kairo — Delete your account and data

App: Kairo: Day Planner & Focus for Android and iPhone · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht)

Delete in the app

  1. Open Kairo and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Account & sharing, then Delete my Kairo account, and confirm.
  3. To also clear this phone: Settings → Your data → Delete all data (uninstalling does the same).

This permanently deletes your Kairo sign-in and everything stored on our server: your profile (name, email and photo), your tasks, lists, comments, photos you added and queued notification emails.

What we keep

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep records. Server backups are overwritten within 30 days. Tasks other people assigned to you stay with them, with your email removed.
