Fastwise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · App: Fastwise / نور الصيام (Android, com.buddhabytes.fasting) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Fastwise works without an account. Your fasts, meals, safety-check answers, weight, waist and glucose readings stay on your phone — there is no Fastwise server, no ads, no analytics or tracking code and no in-app purchases. The only thing that leaves your phone is the city you pick and the date, sent to a prayer-times service so the app can show suhoor and iftar times.

يعمل «نور الصيام» بلا حساب. صيامك ووجباتك وإجابات فحص السلامة والوزن ومحيط الخصر وقراءات السكر تبقى على هاتفك — لا يوجد خادم للتطبيق، ولا إعلانات، ولا أدوات تحليل أو تتبع، ولا مشتريات داخل التطبيق. الشيء الوحيد الذي يغادر هاتفك هو المدينة التي تختارها والتاريخ، ويُرسلان إلى خدمة مواقيت الصلاة لعرض وقتي السحور والإفطار.

Information stored on your phone

To make the app work, Fastwise saves the following only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • your fasting history (start and end times, plan, how it ended) and weekly goal progress;
  • meals you log (name, meal type, calorie and protein/carb/fat estimates) and water glasses;
  • your answers to the safety check (for example age group, pregnancy, diabetes type, GLP-1 medicine, eating-disorder history) and the safety tier worked out from them;
  • optional body and health entries: weight, height, waist and, in Ramadan, blood-glucose readings;
  • Ramadan data (excused days, qada), mood check-ins, badges and your settings (name you type, language, country and city, theme, reminders, quiet hours).

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Fastwise has no account and no sign-in.

Prayer times (Aladhan)

To show suhoor (Fajr) and iftar (Maghrib) times, the Ramadan countdown, the 7-day prayer table and suhoor/iftar reminders, Fastwise asks the free Aladhan prayer-times service for the times. Each request contains the coordinates of the city you chose in the app (a city centre, not your device’s GPS location), the date or month, and your country’s official calculation method. Like any website, Aladhan also receives your phone’s IP address. No name, health data or other personal information is sent. Aladhan’s own privacy policy applies to these requests. If you don’t use Ramadan mode or suhoor/iftar times, no request is made.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics. Fastwise does not use your camera, microphone or GPS.

Reminders

Reminders (suhoor, iftar, water, eating window, fasting goal, sunnah fasts) are notifications scheduled on your phone by Fastwise. No push service or server is involved. The app asks for notification permission, re-schedules reminders after the phone restarts, and never uses exact-alarm permission. Change or turn them off with the bell at the top of the app or in your phone’s notification settings.

Sharing

If you tap Share (for example “Share my week”), Fastwise hands a short text to your phone’s share menu. You choose where it goes. Otherwise, we do not sell, rent or share any information, because we don’t have any.

Delete your data

In Me → Delete my data you can delete everything Fastwise has stored on the phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data. Because there is no account and no server copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android’s own backup to your Google Account, Android may include app data (including Fastwise’s) in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is controlled by you and Google under Google’s privacy policy; Buddha Bytes cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Google Play

Fastwise is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and app crashes under its own privacy policy and shares only aggregated statistics with developers in Play Console.

Health and safety

Fastwise is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Calorie numbers are estimates. If you are pregnant, have diabetes, take insulin or GLP-1 medicine, have a heart or kidney condition or a history of eating disorders, talk to your doctor before fasting. The safety check limits the plans shown, but it is not medical advice.

Children

Fastwise is intended for adults (18+); the safety check does not offer fasting plans to anyone under 18. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps can’t read. Protect it with a screen lock. Prayer-time requests use HTTPS.

Future versions

A future update may add optional features such as an account with cloud backup, AI photo recognition of meals, or a paid subscription (Noor Plus). They will be optional, and we will update this policy before any of them is released.
