Devbhoomi Byoh — Child safety standards

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · App: Devbhoomi Byoh - Matrimony app · Operated by Buddha Bytes · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Devbhoomi Byoh is a matrimony service for adults only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and child marriage.

Our standards

  • Adults only. Members must be legally eligible to marry in India: women at least 18 and men at least 21. Our server rejects profiles below these ages. Completed eligible profiles activate automatically; routine moderation follows. Automatic activation does not verify identity or prove a declared age.
  • No content involving minors. Profiles, photos and messages that sexualise, exploit or endanger anyone under 18, or that seek or arrange a child marriage, are forbidden.
  • Immediate action. A report that a member may be under age hides the profile at once, before review. Accounts involved in CSAE are banned and not allowed back.
  • Reporting to authorities. We report CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities in India, including through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, and cooperate with law enforcement as the law requires. We do not share such material any further.
  • In-app tools. Every profile and chat has Report and Block. Reports go straight to Buddha Bytes reviewers.

How to report

  • In the app: open the profile or chat, tap Report and choose "Looks under age" or the closest reason.
  • By email: support@buddhabytes.app with the subject "Child safety report", the profile name and what you saw. Do not attach or forward abusive images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112 (India) or your local emergency number, or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
