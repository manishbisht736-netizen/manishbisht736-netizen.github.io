Delete your Devbhoomi Byoh account

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · App: Devbhoomi Byoh - Matrimony app · Operated by Buddha Bytes · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Devbhoomi Byoh and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data and confirm.

Deletion starts straight away. Wait for the confirmation in the app. If the connection drops, deletion may already have partly or fully completed. Retry if the app offers it, or email support so we can check the result.

Without the app

Email support@buddhabytes.app from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Devbhoomi Byoh account". We confirm the request with that address and delete the account within 7 days.

Delete some data and keep your account

  • Photos: Me → edit photos → remove a photo. It is deleted from our storage when you save.
  • Caste, gotra or horoscope details: Me → Your data → switch off "Caste and gotra" or "Horoscope details". Those details are deleted from our server.
  • Shared profile visits: Me → Who viewed my profile → switch off visit sharing. Your earlier visit records are deleted.
  • Phone number or other details: edit your profile, clear the field and save.
  • Anything else: email support@buddhabytes.app from your sign-in email and tell us what to delete. We reply within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email, profile, private details, preferences and consent records.
  • All your photos and any verification selfie, from our storage.
  • Your invitations, matches, chat messages, blocks, shared visits, notifications, feedback and review requests.
  • Reports made about you.

What is kept

  • Reports you made about other members stay, without your name, for up to 2 years so we can act on repeat abuse.
  • Moderation log entries (an account identifier, the decision and the date) for up to 2 years, for safety and legal requests.
