Devbhoomi Byoh — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · App: Devbhoomi Byoh - Matrimony app (Android and iPhone) · Operated by Buddha Bytes · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Devbhoomi Byoh is a free matrimony app for adults with roots in Uttarakhand. To introduce families to each other we need some personal details, so we collect only what a match needs, keep sensitive details private until both people say yes, and let you delete everything from the app at any time. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no paid features, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to send sign-in codes. A phone number is optional; it is not verified by SMS, and if you add it, only people you are connected with (you have both accepted) can see it.
  • Profile: name, gender, date of birth, marital status and children, Uttarakhand identity and native district, village and tehsil, where you live now, languages, height and weight, education and work, diet and lifestyle, hobbies, family details, the "about" texts you write and your partner preferences. Who created the profile (self or a family member) is recorded.
  • Only with your separate consent: caste, sub-caste and gotra; birth time and place, manglik status and horoscope details. Without consent these fields are removed on our server, not just hidden.
  • Optional health note you choose to add. It stays private.
  • Photos: up to four profile photos, resized on your phone before upload. You choose who may see them (everyone, verified members or mutual matches).
  • Verification selfie: one private selfie that a Buddha Bytes reviewer compares with your photos. Only the result (verified or not) is kept; the selfie image is deleted after review. A selfie check does not prove identity. We do not collect Aadhaar or identity documents.
  • Activity you create: invitations and responses, matches, chat messages, blocks, reports, feedback, review requests (appeals) and, only if you switch it on, profile visits you share.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to and when.
  • Moderation records: decisions about profiles, reports and appeals, with a short reason.

Who can see what

  • Active members see your name, age, district, city, education, work, income range (unless you choose "Prefer not to say"), lifestyle and about text. Your village, college, phone number, exact date of birth and birth details stay hidden until you and the other person have both accepted.
  • Completed eligible profiles become visible automatically after required details, photos and a private selfie are saved. Buddha Bytes may review profiles afterwards, request corrections or restrict access. Selfie badges require a separate review.
  • Blocked members and people you have reported can no longer see or contact you.
  • Buddha Bytes reviewers can see profiles, photos, selfies waiting for review, reports, feedback and appeals in order to moderate the service.

Why we use it

To run your account and sign you in, show suitable profiles, deliver invitations and chats, keep members safe (review, reports, blocking, suspensions and appeals), answer feedback and grievances, and meet legal obligations. Our basis is your consent and the service you ask us to provide. You can withdraw optional consents in the app; the related details are then removed.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase hosts the database, private photo storage and sign-in, in the Mumbai (India) region.
  • Resend delivers sign-in code emails from noreply@buddhabytes.app.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics or data brokers. Reminders you turn on are scheduled by your phone.

On your phone

The app stores your profile draft, photos you are preparing and your settings on your phone. The camera is used only when you take a photo or the verification selfie. Notifications are optional.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything linked to it are kept until you delete your account.
  • Verification selfies are deleted after review. Read notifications are removed after 30 days (all after 90 days); shared profile visits after 180 days.
  • Closed reports and appeals are kept for up to 2 years and resolved feedback for 1 year, so we can handle repeat abuse and legal requests. Moderation log entries keep only account identifiers and the decision.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, selfie, preferences, invitations, matches, messages, visits and feedback are deleted immediately. Reports you made stay without your name; reports about you are deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • See and edit your profile at any time; edits go back to review.
  • Withdraw consent for caste or horoscope details, visit sharing or reminders in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Me → Delete my account and data) or as described on the account deletion page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by emailing support@buddhabytes.app.

Adults only

The app is only for adults who are eligible to marry (women 18+, men 21+). We do not knowingly collect data about children. Profiles reported as possibly under age are hidden at once until reviewed. See our child safety standards.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Photos and selfies are in private storage and shared only through short-lived links with people allowed to see them. Database rules stop members from reading other people's private fields. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
