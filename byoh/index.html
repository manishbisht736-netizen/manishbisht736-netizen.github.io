Devbhoomi Byoh — Help and support

App: Devbhoomi Byoh - Matrimony app (Android and iPhone) · Operated by Buddha Bytes · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Devbhoomi Byoh is a free matrimony app for adults with roots in Uttarakhand: Garhwali, Kumaoni, Jaunsari and other Pahadi families, in India or abroad.

Contact us

Email support@buddhabytes.app from the email address you sign in with, so we can find your account. You can also send feedback from the app: Me → Send feedback to Buddha Bytes.

To report a profile, open it and tap Report. Reports about a possibly under-age member hide that profile at once until a person reviews it.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, paid unlocks or ads.

Why is my profile not visible yet?

Completed eligible profiles activate automatically after required details, photos and a private selfie are saved. Buddha Bytes may spot-check profiles later. Requests for corrections appear inside the app and do not by themselves hide a profile. Safety restrictions can hide, suspend or ban a profile. Selfie badges require a separate review. You see your profile status in the app.

When can someone see my phone number?

Only after you have both accepted an invitation. Until then your phone number, village, exact date of birth and birth details stay hidden.

How does the kundli guna score work?

If you both choose to share horoscope details, the app shows a guna score out of 36. Kundli is one view, not the whole story; families can always ask their pandit.

I did not get the sign-in code

Codes come from noreply@buddhabytes.app. Check your spam or promotions folder and wait a minute before asking for a new code.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Delete my account and data. Everything is deleted straight away. See Delete account for other options.

Stay safe

A selfie check or email code does not prove who someone is. Meet in public, involve your family, and never send money to someone you met in the app.
