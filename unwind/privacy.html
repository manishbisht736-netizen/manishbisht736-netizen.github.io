Unwind — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · App: Unwind (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Unwind works without an account, and we don’t collect any of your data. Everything you do in Unwind stays on your phone. There is no Unwind server, no ads, no analytics or tracking code, and no in-app purchases. We never see your mood ratings, your sessions or anything you write.

Information stored on your phone

To make the app work, Unwind saves the following only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • the optional before-and-after mood check ratings you choose to give around an activity;
  • your session log (which activity, when, and how long) and the Calm Days, Garden and insights built from it;
  • text you type in reflection activities, such as Worry Lanterns and the Gratitude Jar;
  • Daily Calm results, activity difficulty levels, and your settings (language, theme, sound, haptics, reminders, reduced motion, large text, music-free mode and the crisis-line region).

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Unwind has no account and no sign-in.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone audio, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics. Unwind doesn’t use the camera or the microphone. The crisis-line region is worked out on your phone from its time zone and is never sent anywhere.

On-device safety check

When you write in a reflection activity, Unwind checks the text on your phone for a short list of phrases that can mean someone is not safe (for example, about self-harm). If one is found, Unwind only shows a card with crisis phone numbers. Nothing is sent, saved as a flag or reported to anyone, and this is not a diagnosis.

Crisis lines and links

Crisis numbers in Unwind are phone links: tapping one opens your phone’s dialer with the number filled in, and the call is handled by your phone and your mobile network, not by Unwind. For regions without a listed number, a link opens findahelpline.com in your browser; that website has its own privacy policy. Unwind is not a crisis or emergency service. If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number (for example 999 in the UAE or 112 in India).

Reminders, haptics and sound

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone by Unwind (3 a week by default, never more than 1 a day). No push service or server is involved. You can change or turn them off in Me → Reminders or in your phone’s notification settings. Vibration (haptics) and sounds are made on the phone; sounds are synthesised on the device, not streamed.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a Daily Calm result, Unwind hands a short text (the date, a coloured grid and your number of moves) to your phone’s share menu or copies it to the clipboard. You choose where it goes; it contains no mood data or personal details. Otherwise, we do not sell, rent or share any information, because we don’t have any.

Export and delete

In Me → Privacy you can Export all your Unwind data as a JSON file to your phone, or Delete everything Unwind has stored. Uninstalling Unwind also deletes its data from the phone. See our data deletion page.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android’s own backup to your Google Account, Android may include app data (including Unwind’s) in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is controlled by you and Google under Google’s privacy policy; Buddha Bytes cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

Google Play

Unwind is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and app crashes under its own privacy policy and shares only aggregated statistics (for example install counts and crash reports without personal details) with developers in Play Console.

Health and wellness

Unwind is a general wellness app that helps you relax and manage everyday stress. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and is not a substitute for professional care. If you often feel low, anxious or panicked, please talk to a doctor or a mental-health professional.

Children

Unwind is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone: there is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code, and nothing you type or tap is sent to us or anyone else. Everything stays on the device. Crisis phone numbers, including children’s and young people’s helplines, are always one tap away. Parents and guardians can see or delete everything the app has stored in Me → Privacy, or by uninstalling it. Questions: support@buddhabytes.app.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps can’t read. Protect it with a screen lock. Unwind makes no network requests with your data, so there is nothing to intercept in transit.

Future versions

A future update may add optional features such as breath detection with the microphone, cloud backup or in-app purchases. They will be optional, and we will update this policy before any of them is released.
