Unwind — Delete your data

App: Unwind (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht) · Updated 29 September 2026

Unwind has no account and no server. Your mood check ratings, session log, worries, gratitude notes and settings are stored only on your phone, so Buddha Bytes holds no data about you to delete. You stay in control of it on the device.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Unwind.
  2. Tap the Me tab.
  3. Scroll to Privacy and tap the bin button (Delete everything), then confirm.

This permanently deletes all Unwind data on your phone. It can’t be undone. Tip: tap Export my data first if you want a copy.

Or uninstall

Uninstalling Unwind also removes all of its data from your phone. If you use Android’s backup to your Google Account, an older copy may remain in that backup until Android replaces it; you can manage or delete backups in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

What we keep

Nothing. We never received your data, so there is nothing on our side to keep or delete. If you email us, we keep your email only as long as needed to answer it.

Need help?

Email support@buddhabytes.app with the subject "Unwind data deletion" and we will reply within 7 days. See also our privacy policy.
