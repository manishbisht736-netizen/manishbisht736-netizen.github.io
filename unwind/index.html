Questions

Anything else? Write to support@buddhabytes.app.

Is Unwind free?

Yes. Everything in Unwind is free.

Can Unwind treat anxiety?

No. Unwind is a general wellness app that helps you relax. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you often feel low or anxious, please talk to a doctor.

Does it need the internet?

No. Unwind works offline and keeps your data on your phone.
