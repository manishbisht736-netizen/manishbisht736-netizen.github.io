Humanly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · App: Humanly: Daily Kindness (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Humanly works without an account, and we don’t collect any of your data. Everything you do in Humanly stays on your phone. There is no Humanly server, no ads, no analytics or tracking code, and no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your phone

To make the app work, Humanly saves the following only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • your 30-day journey progress, the kind acts you mark as done, and the garden built from them;
  • the optional one-line reflections and moods you write after an act, and your pledge;
  • cards you save, cards you write yourself, and cards you schedule to send, including the name and the optional WhatsApp number you type for that person;
  • your settings: first name (optional, used only to greet you), who you want to focus on, reminder time, Kindness Day and theme.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. You can export your diary as text or erase everything from Garden → Erase everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Sending cards

When you choose to send a card, Humanly makes the image or text on your phone and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp) through Android’s share sheet, or opens a WhatsApp chat with the number you entered. Humanly doesn’t send messages by itself and doesn’t read your contacts or chats. What happens after that is covered by the app you send with.

Reminders and widget

Reminders are off until you turn them on. They are scheduled on your phone by Android; nothing passes through a server. The home-screen widget shows today’s act from the app’s storage on your phone.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics. Humanly doesn’t use the camera, the microphone or location. Links to the Play Store, findahelpline.com and this page open in your browser and are covered by those sites’ own policies.

Children

Humanly is for a general audience and isn’t designed for children under 13. Cards about kids are written for parents and carers.

Not medical advice

Humanly offers small, everyday kindness ideas. It isn’t therapy or medical care. If you are struggling, please contact a local helpline (findahelpline.com lists free, confidential services) or emergency services.

Changes

If Humanly ever starts sending data off your phone, we will update this policy and the Play Store data-safety answers before that version is released.
