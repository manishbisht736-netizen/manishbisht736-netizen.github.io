Readiness

Know when to push

One Readiness number each morning from your sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and training load, compared with your own 30-day normal. Then one clear thing to do today.

  • Traffic-light dial: green, yellow or red
  • Living orbs for fitness level, sleep and physical age
  • Tap any number for its trend
Sleep debt

See the sleep you owe

Stryka adds up the sleep you missed over the last 14 nights, with recent nights counting more, and shows when you're paying it back.

  • Sleep debt bank with every short night
  • Regularity and weekend shift in plain words
  • Nights with no data are skipped, never counted as short
Trends

Week, month, 3-month and year views for HRV, heart rate, sleep, steps, weight and training, each compared with the weeks before.

  • Patterns explained in plain words, labelled est.

Questions

Anything else? Write to support@buddhabytes.app.

Is Stryka free?

You can try every feature free for 7 days from install, with no card needed. After that, keep the free plan (all running, this week's and last week's insights, Monk, Vibes and breathing stay free) or subscribe to Stryka Premium in the app. Prices are shown clearly before you pay.

Does Stryka work with my watch?

Yes. On Android, Stryka reads steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV and workouts from Health Connect, with your permission. You can also log them yourself. The iPhone version, with Apple Health, is coming soon.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. Pick home or no-equipment plans and Stryka only shows exercises you can do with what you have.

Is Stryka medical advice?

No. Stryka is a wellness app. Calories, health score and other numbers are estimates and not medical advice.
