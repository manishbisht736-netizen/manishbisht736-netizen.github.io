Rise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026 · App: Rise (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Rise is a fitness, food and wellness app. Without a Rise profile, your workouts, food logs and health numbers stay on your phone. If you create a Rise profile to use Sweat Stories and Groups, the things you share (like posts and comments) and a summary of your activity (for example steps, distance and workouts) are stored on our server so other users can see them. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Information stored on your phone

To personalise your plan, Rise saves the following only on your device: your name (optional), age, sex, height, weight and goals; the country you are from or the food style you pick (used only to suggest dishes); workouts, yoga and Monk sessions; food and water logs; health numbers you enter or import (for example steps, sleep, heart rate, weight); photos you choose to add (for example progress photos or food photos); and your settings. Unless you create a Rise profile (see below), this data is not sent to us.

Timer alerts (for example “Rest over” when your screen is locked) are notifications scheduled on your phone by Rise. Nothing about them is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in your phone’s notification settings.

Rise profile, Sweat Stories and Groups (optional)

If you create a Rise profile, we store on our server (Supabase, hosted in the region we select): an account ID, your username, display name and profile photo; your email address (only if you choose to sign in or back up your profile with email — we use it only to send sign-in codes and important account messages, sent via our email provider Resend); if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name and email address with us and we store them the same way; posts, photos, workout summaries, comments, reactions and stories you share; who you follow; groups you create or join and your group activity; notifications and messages sent to you. While you have a profile, Rise also keeps an activity summary on our server so your profile, badges and group leaderboards stay up to date: daily, weekly and monthly steps and distance; workout, run, ride and Monk session counts; personal records and saved routines; and, for groups you join, weekly totals such as points, active minutes, calories burned in workouts, days you hit your water goal, average hours of sleep, the number of days you did a mood check-in (not the mood itself) and your streak. It does not include your age, sex, height, weight, food logs or progress photos. You can set your profile to private in the app. Other Rise users can see your profile, posts and group activity. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. You can delete posts and comments at any time, and delete your whole account in the app.

Safety: reports, blocks and follow requests

If you report a post, comment, profile, message or group, we store the report (what was reported, the reason you chose and your account ID) so our team can review it. Anything reported by three different people is hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. Moderators can hide content and suspend accounts that break the community rules; a suspended account can’t post, comment or send messages, and we keep a record of the suspension and its reason. Reports are private: the person you report can’t see who reported them. If you block someone, the block is saved on your phone and they are removed from the people you follow. If your account is private, follow requests you receive and your answers (accept or decline) are stored on our server so the request can complete.

Invites and referrals

If you have a Rise profile, we store your invite code, and if you joined with a friend’s code, which code you used and whether the invite qualified for a reward (for example, that you completed a first workout within 7 days). To stop reward abuse, the app sends a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your phone’s device ID; we can’t turn it back into the original ID and we don’t use it for ads or tracking. The invite landing page (buddhabytes.app/r/…) doesn’t use cookies or trackers.

Workout links you share

If you have a Rise profile and choose to share a workout, Rise creates a public link (buddhabytes.app/w/…). Anyone with the link can see that workout: its name, minutes, exercises with Rise’s exercise pictures, your first name, your emoji avatar and your invite code. It never includes your body weight, photos or health numbers. Deleting your account deletes your shared links. 3D replay videos are made on your phone and only leave it if you share them.

Features that use the internet

  • Barcode lookup: when you scan a packaged food, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to get the nutrition label.
  • Food database lookup: when Rise cannot find a food in its built-in table, the food name may be sent to the USDA FoodData Central service to get nutrition values.
  • AI food scan and AI coach (when available): if you choose to use these features, the food photo or text you submit is sent securely to our AI service, which passes it to an AI provider (Google Gemini, with Groq or OpenRouter as backups) to identify foods or generate a response. We do not keep these photos or texts after the answer is returned. The providers process them under their own privacy policies.
  • Race calendar: Rise downloads the list of upcoming races from our website (buddhabytes.app/events.json) about once a day. No personal data is sent.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you tap “Continue with Google”, Google’s sign-in page opens in your browser and Google tells us your name and email address. Google’s own privacy policy applies to that page.
  • Links you open (for example "Ask Google AI Mode") open in your browser and are governed by that website's policy.

Location

If you use GPS tracking for a run, walk or ride, Rise uses your location on your phone to measure distance and pace and to draw your route. Your routes are saved on your phone. A route only leaves your phone if you share that activity to Sweat Stories with “Show route map on the post” switched on: then the route (with the first and last 200 metres removed, so your start and finish points are not shown) is stored on our server with the post and other Rise users who can see the post can see the map. Delete the post to delete the route. The list of popular running places is built into Rise and does not send your location anywhere. If you share a route as an image to another app, the image shows only the shape of your route on a plain background (start and finish trimmed) and goes only where you send it. Rise does not load maps from other companies.

Camera and photos

Rise only uses the camera or your photos when you choose to take or add a photo. Photos stay on your phone, except a profile photo or a photo you choose to post in Sweat Stories or Groups (stored on our server as described above), and a food photo you choose to send to the AI food scan.

Health Connect (Android, optional)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Rise reads steps, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, blood-oxygen (SpO₂), weight and exercise sessions (type, duration, distance and calories) from the last 14 days, and shows which app the data came from (for example Samsung Health or Huawei Health through the Health Sync app). If you also turn on “Save my Rise workouts to Health Connect”, Rise writes the estimated calories burned in each Rise workout. These readings are stored on your phone and used only to show your health and activity in Rise. We don’t sell them, use them for advertising, or share them with anyone. If you have a Rise profile, the activity summary described above may include step totals. You can remove Rise’s access at any time in Health Connect settings. Rise’s use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sleep estimated from your phone (Android, optional)

If you turn on “Sleep from your phone”, Rise asks for Android’s “Physical activity” permission and uses Google’s Sleep API (part of Google Play services) to estimate when you fell asleep and woke up, based on your phone’s motion, light and use. Rise only receives the start and end time of each night. These times are stored on your phone and are not uploaded. Like sleep from a watch, they count towards your weekly average sleep hours, which appear on group leaderboard cards only if you join a group (see “Rise profile, Sweat Stories and Groups” above). You can turn it off at any time in Rise (Devices & apps) or remove the permission in Android settings. Sleep from a watch or that you type in always takes priority.

Steps counted by your phone (Android, optional)

If you turn on “Steps from your phone”, Rise uses your phone’s built-in step sensor (with Android’s “Physical activity” permission) to count your steps, about every 15 minutes, even when Rise is closed. Daily step totals are stored on your phone. Like steps from Health Connect, they count towards the step totals shown on your profile badges and on group leaderboards if you have a Rise profile or join a group. Steps from Health Connect, a watch or that you type in always take priority. You can turn it off at any time in Rise (Devices & apps).

Health information

Health scores, calorie estimates, biological age and similar numbers in Rise are estimates for general wellness only. Rise is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet plan, especially if you have a health condition.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Data only leaves your phone for the specific features listed above, and only when you use them.

Children

Rise is intended for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account (or Delete all my data if you never created a profile). This permanently deletes your account and everything you shared from our server, and all Rise data on your phone. See also our account deletion page.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Internet requests made by Rise use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Future versions

Future updates may add cloud backup, email sign-in and watch sync. If they do, we will update this policy first.
