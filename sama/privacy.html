Skycast: privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026 · App ID com.buddhabytes.weather · Published by Buddha Bytes, Dubai, United Arab Emirates · support@buddhabytes.app

In short: Skycast has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location is used only while the app is open, is rounded to about 1 km on your phone, and is sent only to get your forecast. We never sell or share it. We (Buddha Bytes) do not run a server for Skycast and receive none of your data.

All connections use HTTPS. The home-screen widget (Android) refreshes the weather for your saved place about once an hour using the same rounded coordinates. Skycast never uses your location in the background.

What stays on your phone

Your saved places, settings (units, language, morning-brief time) and the last forecast are stored only on your phone, so the app works offline. Uninstalling Skycast or clearing its storage deletes them. There is nothing for us to delete because we never receive it.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app): optional. You can search for a city instead. On Android 12+ you can choose approximate location.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the morning brief (one note a day). The text is made on your phone from the forecast you already have.

Children

Skycast is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Weather and safety

Forecasts, heat-stress estimates, best times and "Skycast guidance" are estimates from weather models, not official warnings and not medical advice. For official warnings, follow your national weather service.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Questions under the UAE PDPL, India's DPDP Act, GDPR, UK GDPR, US state privacy laws or Brazil's LGPD: write to support@buddhabytes.app.

Changes

If Skycast ever adds ads, accounts or anything that sends more data, this page and the store's data-safety answers will be updated before that version is released.
