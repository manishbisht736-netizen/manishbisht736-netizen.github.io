About Buddha Bytes

Buddha Bytes is an independent app studio based in Dubai. We design for genuine, healthy engagement: clear numbers, honest defaults and features people actually asked for, instead of streaks and pressure designed to keep you scrolling.

Our apps are built for the Gulf and South Asia first, with regional food, Ramadan and local languages, and work for anyone, anywhere.
