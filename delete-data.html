Rise — Delete your account and data

App: Rise (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht)

Delete in the app

  1. Open Rise.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right) to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account (or Delete all my data), type DELETE and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Rise account and everything you shared (profile, username, posts, photos, comments, reactions, follows, group memberships, messages) from our server, plus all Rise data on your phone.

What we keep

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep records. Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Need help?

Email support@buddhabytes.app with the subject "Rise data deletion" and we will reply within 7 days.
