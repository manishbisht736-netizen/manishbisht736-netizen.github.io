Stryka — Delete your account and data

App: Stryka (formerly Rise) for Android and iPhone · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht)

Delete in the app

  1. Open Stryka.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right) to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account (or Delete all my data), type DELETE and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Stryka account and everything you shared (profile, username, posts, photos, comments, reactions, follows, group memberships, messages) from our server, plus all Stryka data on your phone.

What we keep

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep records. Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Need help?

Email support@buddhabytes.app with the subject "Stryka data deletion" and we will reply within 7 days.
