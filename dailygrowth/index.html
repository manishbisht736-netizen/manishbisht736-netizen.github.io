Questions

Anything else? Write to support@buddhabytes.app.

Is Daily Growth free?

Yes. Everything is free during launch. Lumi's shop uses Glow you earn, never real money.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. Shields cover missed days automatically, and Never Miss Twice helps you get back on track.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up form. Daily Growth creates an anonymous account for backup, or you can use local-only mode.
