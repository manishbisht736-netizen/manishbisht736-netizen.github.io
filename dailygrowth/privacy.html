Habit Tracker: Daily Growth — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · App: Habit Tracker: Daily Growth (Android, shown as "Daily Growth" on your phone) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht), Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Contact: support@buddhabytes.app

Summary

Daily Growth helps you build small daily habits. Your habits and history live on your phone. To keep a private backup and to let you join Circles with friends, the app creates an anonymous account: no email, phone number or password. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and we never sell your data. You can switch to Local only mode at any time, and then nothing leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your habits, check-ins, timers, counters, routines, quit-habit urge logs, journal entries and notes, moods, Journeys progress, Lumi and badges, and your settings.
  • Your first name, if you type one, so the app can greet you.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. We don't send them from a server.

What is sent to our server (unless you choose Local only)

  • Anonymous account: a random account ID created by our database provider when you first finish setup. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Username: a short name made from your first name plus random letters (for example "manish-ab12"). It is used only so people in your Circles can see who checked in.
  • Private backup: a copy of the app's data listed above, so it can be restored. Journal text, notes and reflections are left out unless you turn on "Include journal text in backup" in Settings. Only your account can read your backup.
  • Circles (optional): if you create or join a Circle (up to 8 people), the members of that Circle can see the shared habit's name, icon and cue, the days each member checked in, members' usernames, and the cheers, nudges or Streak Shields you send each other. Nothing else from your app is shared with them.

Where it is stored

Server data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and protected so each account can only read its own data (Circle members can read only the Circle data described above). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Other services

  • Fonts: the default look uses fonts built into the app. Some optional themes load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when the font is downloaded.
  • Sharing: if you share a progress card, Android's share sheet sends the image only to the app you pick.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the habit reminders you set. By default at most one gentle nudge a day, never in your quiet hours.
  • Internet: for the backup and Circles. Not needed in Local only mode.
  • Vibration and keep screen on: for check-in feedback and while a timer is running.

Your choices

  • Local only: Settings → Privacy & data → Local only stops all syncing and Circles.
  • Export: Settings → Privacy & data → Export JSON or CSV.
  • Delete: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my data & account. See how deletion works.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your backup and Circle data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, backup, username, Circle check-ins and cheers are deleted from our server right away. Circles you created are removed for all members. Server backups held by our provider are overwritten within 30 days.

Children

Daily Growth is a general-audience app for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Wellbeing information

Lessons and Journeys share general habit and wellbeing information. They are not medical, mental-health or addiction treatment advice. For a health condition, addiction or a crisis, please talk to a professional or a local helpline.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@buddhabytes.app. We reply within 7 days.
