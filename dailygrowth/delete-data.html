Habit Tracker: Daily Growth — Delete your account and data

App: Habit Tracker: Daily Growth (Android) · Developer: Buddha Bytes (Manish Singh Bisht)

Delete in the app

  1. Open Habit Tracker: Daily Growth.
  2. Tap your initial (top right on Today) to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Privacy & data and tap Delete my data & account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your anonymous account and everything linked to it on our server (private backup, username, Circle memberships, check-ins and cheers), plus all app data on your phone. Circles you created are removed for all members. If you want a copy first, use Export JSON or Export CSV on the same screen.

What we keep

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep records. Server backups held by our database provider are overwritten within 30 days.

Can't open the app?

Email support@buddhabytes.app with the subject "Daily Growth data deletion" and your username (Settings → Privacy & data shows it). We will delete your data and reply within 7 days.
